Before we go

Bring with us

🚕 Taxis & Apps

  • Download FreeNow & IT Taxi; Uber works in Rome/Florence only.
  • FCO → Rome fixed fare €50; use official white-taxi ranks only.
  • Rural Tuscany & Cetara: no street taxis – hotel reception must call one (15–30 min notice).
  • Train doors close 1 min early; find your train number on departure boards.

🧳 Bags & Wine

  • Basic airfare = carry-on included; pre-pay checked bag online.
  • Wine bottles must go in checked luggage. Canada allowance: 1.5 L wine/adult duty-free.

🗣️ Handy Italian

  • Buongiorno = good morning / hello · Buonasera = good evening · Grazie mille = thank you very much
  • Per favore = please · Scusi = excuse me · Mi dispiace = I’m sorry · Parla inglese? = do you speak English?
  • Un tavolo per due, per favore = a table for two, please
  • Il conto, per favore = the bill, please · Possiamo pagare con la carta? = can we pay by card?
  • Acqua naturale / frizzante = still / sparkling water · Un caffè, per favore = a coffee, please
  • Dov’è il bagno? = where is the bathroom? · Dov’è la stazione? = where is the station?
  • Quanto costa? = how much is it? · È troppo caro = it’s too expensive
  • Abbiamo una prenotazione = we have a reservation · La camera è pronta? = is the room ready?
  • Due biglietti per Roma = two tickets to Rome · Da quale binario? = from which platform?
  • Aiuto, per favore = help, please · Ho bisogno di una farmacia = I need a pharmacy
